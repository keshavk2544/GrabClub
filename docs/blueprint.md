# **App Name**: Campus Cart

## Core Features:

- Marketplace Listing: Allow local vendors to list food, groceries, fruits, vegetables, sweets, bakery items, and medicines available for sale, with details, images, and real-time inventory. All store and product data is saved on the Firestore database.
- Order Placement: Enable students to browse the listings, add items to their cart, and place orders for delivery or pickup.
- Real-time Order Tracking: Provide real-time updates on order status (e.g., processing, en route, delivered).
- Delivery Coordination: Connect delivery personnel with vendors and students to ensure timely and efficient delivery. Delivery information is saved on the Firestore database.
- Secure Payment Gateway: Integrate a secure payment gateway for processing transactions, supporting various payment methods.
- Vendor Management: Provide a dashboard for vendors to manage their listings, track orders, and update their store information.
- Personalized Recommendations: Use collaborative filtering to generate personalized product recommendations based on students' past orders and browsing history. Use of tool to help filter out poor quality results.

## Style Guidelines:

- Base color: Deep black background (#0D0D0D).
- Primary accent: Vibrant orange-gold (#FE9D15) for highlights, buttons, and icons.
- Secondary accents: Electric blue (#00C6FF), magenta (#FF3D7F), and lime green (#A8FF60) for gradients and highlights.
- Text colors: Primary text (#FFFFFF), Secondary text (#B0B0B0).
- Headlines: 'Poppins' (geometric sans-serif), bold.
- Body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif), medium-weight, high readability.
- Clean, vibrant, and minimal layout with dynamic shadows and glass-like surfaces, implementing glassmorphism and neumorphism.
- Rounded cards and large touch targets for ease of use.
- Fluid transitions between sections (e.g., fade + slide animations), and smooth micro-interactions for a modern, youthful feel.
